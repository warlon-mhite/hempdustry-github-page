Hempdustry 2

(formerly GanjaCraft)

The cannabis mod for Minecraft, for stoners and hemprepreneurs. Grow weed in a field or under a lamp, smoke it, press it into hash, bake it into space cake, then build a fireproof house out of the leftovers.

A quick word first. Hempdustry is a comedy (but serious) mod about a plant. Nobody here is trying to teach you how to roll, sift or press anything in real life, and nothing in this mod is medical advice. It's blocks, items, and a couple of dumb jokes about the munchies.

Grow your own

Three strains, three very different plants.

Purple Kush

Short and leafy, two blocks tall. Grows wild in jungles, swamps and lush caves.

Lemon Haze

Three blocks tall, slower, wants headroom. Pays out in stems. Grows wild from savanna to badlands.

Beldía

The Rif's landrace, the plant Moroccan hash came from. Won't touch farmland: it wants sand with water close by. Wild on desert riverbanks, seeds in desert temples and suspicious sand.

  • Trim it. Shear a growing plant twice and the harvest leans toward buds. Or don't, it grows anyway.
  • Find it. Seeds drop from tall grass and turn up in chests and village houses. Wild plants are rare, shear one up for a flower pot.
  • Farm life. Bees pollinate it. Chickens and parrots eat the seeds. Goats go for the leaves. And now and then a creeper wanders into a grown field and hides in it, so wall and light the big ones.

Grow it indoors

More buds, faster, and more ways to mess it up.

  1. Grow Pot

    Water it with a bottle, feed it bone meal. Half again as fast as a good field, and pots pack wall to wall.

  2. Grow Lamp

    Hangs on copper chains, switches on like a redstone lamp. Two extra buds for every plant under it.

  3. Hydro Tray

    Water, fertiliser and a redstone signal for the pump. Twice as fast as the best field.

The plant remembers its light. Kill the lamp while it's flowering and it stresses: the colour drains out, the old leaves go brown, and you lose buds. A dry pot, a stopped pump or bone meal on a flowering plant can do it too.

A stressed plant also spoils some of its buds into schwag. Seedy, stemmy, weak. Smoke it and you'll probably just get poisoned. There's an advancement for that.

Pack a bowl

The device decides how long. The buds decide how hard.

  • Spliff

    Roll it, smoke it, gone. Crumble in some hash for a stronger one.

  • Wooden pipe

    Pack it again and again. Takes enchantments, repairs on an anvil.

  • Bong

    Bigger bowl, up to three buds. Hold the button and it bubbles while you draw.

  • Redstone Vaporizer

    One bud, level I, lasts longer than a pipe. Can't green you out.

Bongs come in all sixteen stained-glass colors and you can set one down on a table. It's for show: pick it back up to smoke it.

Hashish

  • Night Vision
  • Resistance
  • Slowness
  • Munchies

More buds, stronger high, bigger downside. Buffs stop at level II like a beacon. The one way to III: pair a bud with a concentrate, a pinch of hash in a spliff or a moon rock in a bong.

Greening out is real. Go big and you might lose the hit and spend a while nauseous instead. At three it also wipes every buff you had and benches you for a minute. A single bud never does that.

Make hash

The old ways, the ones that only need a screen, cold water, heat and pressure. No BHO, not happening.

  1. Sifting Box

    Fill it like a composter. Seven buds in, nine kief out. Sift the kief again for a cleaner grade.

  2. Ice wash

    Fill the same box with water, put ice on all four sides: bubble hash. Plain ice melts if you light the room.

  3. Hemp Press

    A screw press heated from below. Kief becomes hashish, the clean stuff becomes rosin.

  4. Rosin

    The strongest thing in the mod. One piece fills a bong.

  • Hash bars

    Nine pieces pack into a bar you can put on a shelf. Cut pieces off with a sword, five cuts, and watch it shrink.

  • Charas

    Shear a ripe plant and sometimes rub off a pinch. The plant lives. Slow Falling.

  • Moon rocks

    A bud, rosin and a coat of hash or kief. Bong only, and it reaches level III.

  • Five kinds

    Kief, bubble hash, hashish, charas, rosin. Hold all five and there's an advancement in it.

Hash is for smoking only. The kitchen still runs on plant matter.

The kitchen

Real cannabutter, made the long way. Edibles are the endgame, not a day-one snack.

  1. Decarboxylator

    A hemp-brick oven with three trays.

  2. Cauldron

    Rinse it. Optional, but it makes better butter.

  3. Infuser

    Pour in milk, light a fire under it, wait.

  4. Cannabutter

    Strength and quality both count. Patience pays.

  • Space Cookie
  • Space Brownie
  • Space Cake
  • Cannabutter Toast
  • Dawamesk
  • Bucket of Bhang

One batch of butter makes eight cookies or a single dawamesk. Guess which one hits harder.

Edibles take thirty seconds to three minutes to kick in, and the worse the butter, the less you can predict it. Eating a second one because the first "isn't working" is a mistake the game will let you make.

Burnt a leaf in the furnace by accident? That's scorched hemp. The Infuser takes it, but four count as one and the butter never gets past the weakest grade.

For server owners and pack makers
  • Fabric or NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.11 or 1.21.1. Install it on both client and server. The 1.21.1 builds have everything except what that version can't hold: the shelf, the harness and canvas bundles.
  • config/hempdustry.json can switch off every drug effect ("industrial hemp only"), green-outs, nausea, loot and the wandering creepers, or speed up crops and machines. /hempdustry reload applies it.
  • Strains (hash included) are a datapack registry. Edit the shipped ones or add your own. They load with the world, not on /reload.
  • The Decarboxylator, the Infuser and the Hemp Press run on datapack recipes, so KubeJS and CraftTweaker can rebalance them. Grow pots and trays take anything in #c:fertilizers.
  • The Infuser and the Hemp Press pour into chests, hoppers and anything with storage, AE2 included. Dispensers can water pots and fill trays.
  • Addon events (smoke, eat, infuse, and a veto) on both loaders.
  • A world from any Hempdustry 2 version opens on every later one. Moving a world between Fabric and NeoForge isn't promised.

Who's behind this and the wacky story of the mod

I go by Warlon Mhite. I've been playing Minecraft since beta 1.7.4, got into modding thanks to Pipix (if memory serves), and started tinkering with game files as a kid thanks to Minecraft texture packs, long before I touched any actual code.

During the COVID pandemic, bored and admittedly a bit of a stoner myself, I noticed there wasn't a decent cannabis mod for any recent Minecraft version. MCreator made that gap easy to fill. I put together a rough but working Forge mod called GanjaCraft. It picked up a small and genuinely enthusiastic crowd.

After that, I teamed up with a school friend on Brewevolution, a beer and tavern RP mod that did a lot better than GanjaCraft ever did, partly thanks to bstylia14's video showcasing it at 4:20 (I don't think that's a coincidence). That's also where MCreator's limits started showing. Around the same time my life got busy in ways that had nothing to do with Minecraft, and both mods went quiet for a while.

Somewhere in there, NotTyzo rewrote GanjaCraft for its Reforged Network server. He picked up the cannabis-mod idea and rewrote it properly in Java, starting for 1.16 and quickly bringing it up to speed with newer versions like 1.18, all with my permission, and even worked with me on some details. Around that time the mod got banned from CurseForge for having "ganja" in the name. It thus got renamed to Hempdustry, and shortly after became Hemp-Craft, which is still maintained under various mod loaders.

In 2025 I started my own from-scratch rewrite for Fabric 1.21, mostly to relearn real Java modding, and after another long gap decided to actually finish it properly. Hempdustry 2 is that mod: the name is a small nod to the version that came after mine and didn't make it. Since 2.1 it runs on NeoForge too, so it's back on the Forge side of the fence where GanjaCraft started.

While Hemp-Craft and Hempdustry 2 share some mechanics and textures, the two mods are completely different projects now. Hemp-Craft is not published under a Free Software license. NotTyzo worked hard on reimplementing all my ideas without MCreator, and had my authorization to use the textures and initial CurseForge page and branding. During my rewrite of Hempdustry 2, I changed multiple things, reworking some mechanics and adding new ones to make the mod more complete and more aligned with real-world usage and science. But of course, this is still a Minecraft mod, so it's nowhere near realistic.

FAQ

  • Fabric or NeoForge?

    Both, since 2.1.0. Same mod, same blocks, same numbers. Each loader has its own download for Minecraft 1.21.11 and for 1.21.1, so pick the one that matches your game.

  • Do I need any other mods for this to work?

    On Fabric, just Fabric API, on both client and server. On NeoForge, nothing. JEI and REI are optional and get their own recipe pages on both loaders, EMI too on 1.21.1. It's built to not fight with the usual kitchen-sink modpacks.

  • Is it stable?

    Yes. 2.1.0 is a stable release on both loaders, played in a real client on all four builds before it went out. A world made with any version of Hempdustry 2 opens on every later one, with nothing for you to do. Bug reports are still welcome.

  • Is there a weed mod for Bedrock, Forge or older versions?

    Hempdustry 2 is Java Edition only, on Fabric and NeoForge for 1.21.11 and 1.21.1. No Bedrock or Pocket Edition. The old Forge builds for 1.16.5 and 1.18 are still on the CurseForge page, but they're a different mod from this one.

  • Can I have the hemp without the weed?

    Yes. Set effects.enabled to false in config/hempdustry.json and nothing gets anyone high, green-outs included. The farming, building and food all stay.

  • What happened to GanjaCraft?

    Same mod, new name. Platforms weren't fond of "ganja" in a listing title, so this rewrite ships as Hempdustry 2. The old GanjaCraft still exists as a historical footnote, not a separate project.

  • Can I use this in my modpack?

    Yes, monetized packs included, as long as the launcher downloads it. Mirroring, modifying and forking are fine too, with credit, non-commercially. Only a commercial use needs to ask. CREDITS.md has the details.
