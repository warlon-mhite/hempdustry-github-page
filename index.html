Hempdustry 2

(formerly GanjaCraft)

The cannabis mod for Minecraft, for stoners and hemprepreneurs. Grow weed in a field or under a lamp, smoke it, press it into hash, bake it into space cake, then build a fireproof house out of the leftovers.

A quick word first. Hempdustry is a comedy (but serious) mod about a plant. Nobody here is trying to teach you how to roll, sift or press anything in real life, and nothing in this mod is medical advice. It's blocks, items, and a couple of dumb jokes about the munchies.

Grow your own

Three strains, three very different plants.

Purple Kush

Short and leafy, two blocks tall. Grows wild in jungles, swamps and lush caves.

Lemon Haze

Three blocks tall, slower, wants headroom. Pays out in stems. Grows wild from savanna to badlands.

Beldía

The Rif's landrace, the plant Moroccan hash came from. Won't touch farmland: it wants sand with water close by. Wild on desert riverbanks, seeds in desert temples and suspicious sand.

  • Trim it. Shear a growing plant twice and the harvest leans toward buds. Or don't, it grows anyway.
  • Find it. Seeds drop from tall grass and turn up in chests and village houses. Wild plants are rare, shear one up for a flower pot.
  • Farm life. Bees pollinate it. Chickens and parrots eat the seeds. Goats go for the leaves. And now and then a creeper wanders into a grown field and hides in it, so wall and light the big ones.

Grow it indoors

More buds, faster, and more ways to mess it up.

  1. Grow Pot

    Water it with a bottle, feed it bone meal. Half again as fast as a good field, and pots pack wall to wall.

  2. Grow Lamp

    Hangs on copper chains, switches on like a redstone lamp. Two extra buds for every plant under it.

  3. Hydro Tray

    Water, fertiliser and a redstone signal for the pump. Twice as fast as the best field.

The plant remembers its light. Kill the lamp while it's flowering and it stresses: the colour drains out, the old leaves go brown, and you lose buds. A dry pot, a stopped pump or bone meal on a flowering plant can do it too.

A stressed plant also spoils some of its buds into schwag. Seedy, stemmy, weak. Smoke it and you'll probably just get poisoned. There's an advancement for that.

Pack a bowl

The device decides how long. The buds decide how hard.

  • Spliff

    Roll it, smoke it, gone. Crumble in some hash for a stronger one.

  • Wooden pipe

    Pack it again and again. Takes enchantments, repairs on an anvil.

  • Bong

    Bigger bowl, up to three buds. Hold the button and it bubbles while you draw.

  • Redstone Vaporizer

    One bud, level I, lasts longer than a pipe. Can't green you out.

Bongs come in all sixteen stained-glass colors and you can set one down on a table. It's for show: pick it back up to smoke it.

More buds, stronger high, bigger downside. Buffs stop at level II like a beacon. The one way to III: pair a bud with a concentrate, a pinch of hash in a spliff or a moon rock in a bong.

Greening out is real. Go big and you might lose the hit and spend a while nauseous instead. At three it also wipes every buff you had and benches you for a minute. A single bud never does that.

Make hash

The old ways, the ones that only need a screen, cold water, heat and pressure. No BHO, not happening.

  1. Sifting Box

    Fill it like a composter. Seven buds in, nine kief out. Sift the kief again for a cleaner grade.

  2. Ice wash

    Fill the same box with water, put ice on all four sides: bubble hash. Plain ice melts if you light the room.

  3. Hemp Press

    A screw press heated from below. Kief becomes hashish, the clean stuff becomes rosin.

  4. Rosin

    The strongest thing in the mod. One piece fills a bong.

  • Hash bars

    Nine pieces pack into a bar you can put on a shelf. Cut pieces off with a sword, five cuts, and watch it shrink.

  • Charas

    Shear a ripe plant and sometimes rub off a pinch. The plant lives. Slow Falling.

  • Moon rocks

    A bud, rosin and a coat of hash or kief. Bong only, and it reaches level III.

  • Five kinds

    Kief, bubble hash, hashish, charas, rosin. Hold all five and there's an advancement in it.

Hash is for smoking only. The kitchen still runs on plant matter.

The kitchen

Real cannabutter, made the long way. Edibles are the endgame, not a day-one snack.

  1. Decarboxylator

    A hemp-brick oven with three trays.

  2. Cauldron

    Rinse it. Optional, but it makes better butter.

  3. Infuser

    Pour in milk, light a fire under it, wait.

  4. Cannabutter

    Strength and quality both count. Patience pays.

  • Space Cookie
  • Space Brownie
  • Space Cake
  • Cannabutter Toast
  • Dawamesk
  • Bucket of Bhang

One batch of butter makes eight cookies or a single dawamesk. Guess which one hits harder.

Edibles take thirty seconds to three minutes to kick in, and the worse the butter, the less you can predict it. Eating a second one because the first "isn't working" is a mistake the game will let you make.

Burnt a leaf in the furnace by accident? That's scorched hemp. The Infuser takes it, but four count as one and the butter never gets past the weakest grade.

Only on Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3

Minecraft 26.1 and 26.2 brought sulfur cubes, potent sulfur, named times for /time and armour cut to a baby's size. The 26.x builds put all of it to work, so these come with the 26.2 and 26.3 downloads only, on Fabric and NeoForge.

  • The footbag

    Feed a hemp bale to a large sulfur cube. It stops bouncing, floats, and a punch pops it up about two blocks instead of away. Get a few friends and keep it in the air. Shears take the bale back.

  • Powdery mildew

    Hemp under a roof, glass included, can catch it. White powder, drifting spores, and it spreads to the plant next door. A quarter of a sick plant's buds come out as schwag. Fields never get it.

  • Jelly legs

    A green-out takes the ground from under you. Your feet slide when you stop and a hit sends you further. Standing still is safe. A cliff edge isn't.

  • 4:20 on the clock

    /time set hempdustry:four_twenty, right next to day, noon and night.

  • The Munchies

    Advancement number 43: eat something while you're under Hunger and too hungry to sprint. A good session and an empty fridge will do it.

  • Baby sizes

    A baby zombie, husk, drowned or piglin that picks up the hemp outfit wears it cut to its own size, dye and all, like it does with leather.

  • Other mods' farmland

    Any soil another mod puts in Minecraft's crop tags takes hemp seeds and grows them like it grows wheat. Nothing to set up.

On 26.3, REI works on NeoForge too (with Architectury API 22.0.5 or newer), and NeoForge kicks a footbag as hard as Fabric does. On NeoForge 26.2 a footbag gets about half the kick, and jelly legs slide without the extra knockback. A world moved up to 26.2 or 26.3 keeps everything it had. The wiki has the numbers.

Sulfur against mildew. Shears clear a sick plant in a trim window. Or put Minecraft's own potent sulfur under water: no plant its gas reaches catches mildew. Gas a plant while it flowers, though, and a tenth of its buds turn to schwag anyway, so take the water away once the flowers show.

For servers and modpacks

Every mechanic has a switch, the machines talk to pipes, and the recipes and strains are plain data. Built to sit in a kitchen-sink pack without a fight.

One file, every switch

Read by the server (one small client section aside), with the same keys on every loader and version. A value out of range is pulled back into it and logged.

  • Effects on or off
  • Green-outs
  • Nausea
  • Munchies
  • Every debuff
  • Effect level caps
  • Effect length
  • Cooldowns
  • Edible onset
  • Crop growth speed
  • Machine speed
  • Infuser times
  • Loot
  • Creepers in fields
  • Mildew (26.x)
  • Update notice (client)

Ready-made presets (hemp only, no green-outs, fast farming, no loot) are on the Server Guide.

Build your config file

Every key in config/hempdustry.json, the same on every loader and version. Change what you need: the file keeps only that, and the mod fills in the rest at its defaults. Every key is explained on the Configuration page.

  • Same jar, both sides

    The server and every player run the same file. Fabric needs Fabric API, NeoForge and Forge nothing. Java 25 on 26.x, 21 on 1.21, 17 on 1.18.2, 8 on 1.16.5. Pick the file.

  • Recipes are data

    Every recipe, the Decarboxylator, Infuser and Hemp Press included, is a datapack recipe. KubeJS, CraftTweaker or a plain datapack can rebalance any of them.

  • Strains are data

    Strains, hash included, are a datapack registry. Edit the shipped ones or add your own. They load with the world, not on /reload.

  • Pipes and hoppers

    The Infuser and the Hemp Press pour into chests, hoppers and any storage, AE2 included. Dispensers water pots, fill trays and pour milk into the Infuser.

  • Other mods' stuff

    Grow pots and trays take anything in #c:fertilizers. Convention tags, compost and fuel hook into the rest of a pack with no code.

  • JEI on a server

    Since 1.21.2 a server doesn't send players its recipes, so on 1.21.11 and 26.x JEI shows the crafting recipes only if it's on the server too. The machine pages show either way.

  • Addon events

    Smoke, eat and infuse events, and a veto, on Fabric and on NeoForge. What addons build on stays put from one version to the next.

  • Worlds keep

    A world from any Hempdustry 2 version opens on every later one. A GanjaCraft or Hemp-Craft server converts on the Forge builds. Moving between Fabric and NeoForge isn't promised.

  • In your modpack

    Allowed, monetized packs included, as long as the launcher downloads it. Set client.updateCheck to false so players aren't told about updates the pack can't take yet.

Minecraft 1.16.5 and GanjaCraft

On CurseForge, filter by your Minecraft version and take the file named as above. The older GanjaCraft files still listed there are a different mod.

  1. Install the loader for your Minecraft version: Fabric, NeoForge or Forge. Most launchers do it for you.
  2. On Fabric only, put Fabric API in your mods folder.
  3. Drop the Hempdustry jar in mods and start the game. A Hempdustry tab in the creative inventory means it loaded.

On a server, the same jar goes on the server and on every player's game. Updating is swapping the jar: a world from any Hempdustry 2 version opens on every later one, nothing to do.

Got an old GanjaCraft or Hemp-Craft world?

Keep it. The Forge builds open it and turn it into a Hempdustry world as it loads. No tool to run, nothing to set up.

GanjaCraft, Minecraft 1.16.5

Every Forge 1.16.5 build opens: the original BETA v0.4 to v0.5.1 and NotTyzo's 1.0.0 to 1.0.6. Crops keep their stage, blocks keep their shape, signs keep their text, and a packed bong stays packed.

From GanjaCraft to Hempdustry 2

A lockdown project, a ban over one word, a fork that went its own way and a rewrite from scratch. The wacky story of the mod, told by the one who made it.

  1. Beta 1.7.4

    Texture packs first

    I go by Warlon Mhite. I've played Minecraft since Beta 1.7.4 and was tinkering with its files as a kid, through texture packs, long before I touched any real code. Pipix got me into modding, if memory serves.

  2. 2021 · Forge 1.16.5

    GanjaCraft

    During the COVID pandemic, bored and admittedly a bit of a stoner, I noticed there wasn't a decent cannabis mod for any recent Minecraft. MCreator made that gap easy to fill. GanjaCraft was rough, but it worked, and it found a small and genuinely enthusiastic crowd.

    Still got a GanjaCraft world? It opens.

  3. Next

    Brewevolution, and a quiet spell

    Then came Brewevolution, a beer and tavern RP mod made with a school friend. It did a lot better than GanjaCraft ever did, partly thanks to bstylia14's video showcasing it at 4:20 (I don't think that's a coincidence). It's also where MCreator's limits started showing. Life got busy in ways that had nothing to do with Minecraft, and both mods went quiet for a while.

  4. 2022–2023 · Forge 1.16 to 1.18

    Hemp-Craft

    Meanwhile NotTyzo rewrote GanjaCraft properly in Java for the Reforged Network server, with my permission, starting on 1.16 and bringing it up to 1.18, and even worked with me on some details. Then CurseForge banned the mod for having "ganja" in its name. It became Hempdustry, and shortly after Hemp-Craft, which is still maintained on several loaders.

    Hemp-Craft worlds open too.

    2. Hempdustry

  5. 2025 · Fabric 1.21

    Hempdustry 2

    In 2025 I started my own rewrite from scratch on Fabric 1.21, mostly to relearn real Java modding, and after another long gap decided to actually finish it properly. The name is a small nod to the Hempdustry that came after mine and didn't make it.

    Back where it started

    Since 2.1 it runs on NeoForge too, so it's back on the Forge side of the fence where GanjaCraft started, and on old Forge itself for 1.16.5 and 1.18.2, where it picks up a GanjaCraft or Hemp-Craft world right where you left it.

Two mods, one root

Hemp-Craft and Hempdustry 2 share some mechanics and textures, but they're completely different projects now. NotTyzo worked hard on reimplementing my ideas without MCreator, and had my go-ahead to use the textures and the original CurseForge page and branding. Hemp-Craft is not published under a free software licence. In Hempdustry 2 I reworked some mechanics and added new ones, to make the mod more complete and closer to real-world use and science. It's still a Minecraft mod, though, so it's nowhere near realistic.

FAQ

  • Fabric, NeoForge or Forge?

    All three. Fabric and NeoForge on Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 1.21.11 and 1.21.1, Forge on 1.18.2 and 1.16.5. Same mod, same blocks, same numbers. Each loader has its own file for each version, so pick the one that matches your game: the download picker lists them all.

  • Which Minecraft version should I pick?

    Whichever your world or modpack is on. Every build has the whole mod, minus what its Minecraft can't hold. 26.2 and 26.3 add a few extras only the new Minecraft can do, 1.21.1 misses the shelf, the harness and canvas bundles, and 1.18.2 and 1.16.5 miss a few more, listed with each file. Coming from GanjaCraft or Hemp-Craft, take the Forge build that opens your world. Moving a world up a version keeps everything in it.

  • Do I need any other mods for this to work?

    On Fabric, just Fabric API, on both client and server. On NeoForge and Forge, nothing. JEI and REI are optional and get their own recipe pages, EMI too on 1.21.1. On NeoForge 26.2 and on 1.16.5 it's JEI only, since REI doesn't run there. It's built to not fight with the usual kitchen-sink modpacks.

  • Is it stable?

    Yes. 2.1.0 is a stable release on all three loaders, played in a real client on every build before it went out. A world made with any version of Hempdustry 2 opens on every later one, with nothing for you to do. Bug reports are still welcome.

  • Is there a weed mod for Bedrock, Forge or older versions?

    Hempdustry 2 is Java Edition only: Fabric and NeoForge for 26.3, 26.2, 1.21.11 and 1.21.1, and Forge for 1.18.2 and 1.16.5. No Bedrock or Pocket Edition, and no build for the versions in between. The CurseForge page also still holds the old GanjaCraft files for 1.16.5 and 1.18; they're a different mod. The Hempdustry files are the ones named hempdustry-….

  • Can I have the hemp without the weed?

    Yes. Set effects.enabled and effects.greenOut to false in config/hempdustry.json and nothing gets anyone high or greens anyone out. The farming, building and food all stay. More switches.

  • What happened to GanjaCraft?

    Same mod, new name. Platforms weren't fond of "ganja" in a listing title, so this rewrite ships as Hempdustry 2. The old GanjaCraft still exists as a historical footnote, not a separate project. Still got a GanjaCraft world, or a Hemp-Craft one? The Forge builds open it and convert it.

  • Can I use this in my modpack?

    Yes, monetized packs included, as long as the launcher downloads it. Mirroring, modifying and forking are fine too, with credit, non-commercially. Only a commercial use needs to ask. CREDITS.md has the details.
